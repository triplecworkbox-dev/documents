Card 2 · Who We're Calling
Before You Start
Your list today

Friendly faces, gone quiet

Everyone on this list has already expressed interest in Opportunity before — as a member, a past volunteer, or someone who signed up and never got a follow-up.

No cold energy needed. This is a warm reconnection.

Card 3 · Our Goals
Two outcomes we want

Simple call, simple goal

1
Get them to one upcoming volunteering session
2
Update the database — busy, keen, or no more calls

That's it. Don't overload the call with anything else.

Card 5 · Step 1
The ScriptStep 1
Open warm

Remind them why they signed up

You say
"Hi name! This is you, a volunteer with the Opportunity Party. You signed up with us a while back — I wanted to check in and see how you're doing."
Card 6 · Step 1 continued
Golden rule

No guilt, ever

Life gets busy. Don't apologise on their behalf, and don't make them explain why it's been a while.

You say
"No matter your experience, or how long it's been since your last event, we'll make sure you have everything you need to jump back in."
Card 7 · Step 2
The ScriptStep 2
Make the ask

Give them one clear next step

You say
"We've got an event coming up on date at location — no experience needed. Would you be up for coming along?"

One event, one clear invite. Don't list five options.

Card 8 · Branch
If yes

Lock it in immediately

You say
"Brilliant! I'll text you the sign-up link right now so it's easy to find later."

Send the text before you hang up — momentum fades fast.

Card 9 · Branch
If busy right now

Keep the door open

You say
"No worries at all — I'll send you the sign-up link so you can find something that fits whenever you're ready."

Note it in the system as "keen, sent link" — this is still a good outcome.

Card 10 · Branch
If not interested anymore

Thank them, close the loop

You say
"Totally understand. Thanks so much for everything you've already given us — have a great day."

Update their record so we don't call again unnecessarily.

Card 11 · Step 3
ClosingStep 3
✓

Wrap it up right

  • Send the text before you move to your next call
  • Log the outcome — keen, busy, or not interested
  • Thank them either way. They said yes once already.
Card 12 · Outro

brings someone back
