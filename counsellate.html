A note from Aniké

My name is Aniké Jenneker. I am a Coloured South African currently living in Wellington, New Zealand, with a life that has spanned South Africa, China, Dubai, Australia, and now here. I am reaching out because I am ready to commit to genuine therapeutic support — and I want to be honest from the beginning, which is something I have not always managed to do.

I am carrying a great deal at once: the weight of cultural displacement and identity, significant financial stress, complicated family relationships, and the pressure of building multiple ventures simultaneously. I am also exploring the possibility that I may be neurodivergent, and I process the world better through reading and writing than through unstructured verbal conversation.

What I need most is a therapist who can hold the full complexity of what I am building and surviving — without asking me to scale down or simplify. These questions are not a test. They are an invitation to a real conversation about whether we are a good fit.
