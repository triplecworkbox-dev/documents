Card 3 · Before Every Call
Before Every Call
✓

Get set before
you dial

  • Write down their name
  • Check if we already have their email
  • Know 3 upcoming events — one volunteering, one meet-up
  • Smile. They can hear it.
Card 4 · Step 1
The ScriptStep 1
Open with "hello"

Introduce yourself & ask permission

You say
"Hi! Am I speaking with [NAME]? My name's [YOU], and I'm a volunteer for the Opportunity Party. Do you have a few minutes to talk?"
Card 5 · Step 1 Branch
If They're BusyStep 1
No time to talk?

That's ok — offer a text instead

You say
"No worries! If it's alright, I'll send a quick text with some upcoming events. Thanks — have a good day."

Never push. Say goodbye warmly.

Card 6 · Step 2
The ScriptStep 2
Ask questions

Listen 80% of the time

You ask
"How do you feel about us as a party — what would you like to see us focus on more?"

One question, then follow-up questions. Don't run a checklist.
