Card 3 · The Single Ask
One clear decision, not a menu

Ask this, and only this

You say
"Are you willing and able to call 10 other people?"

Not "would you like to volunteer, or become a member, or..." — just this one question. A clear yes or no is easier to give than a choice.

Card 4 · Step 1

Card 5 · Branch
If yes

Send the text immediately

You say
"Brilliant! I'll text you the FieldEdge link right now."

Don't wait — send it while you're still on the call. See Card 8 for the exact routine.

Card 6 · Branch
If unsure or busy

Still send the link

You say
"No worries — I'll send the link anyway in case it works out."

Log it as "sent link, unsure" — this still counts as a good outcome.

Card 7 · Branch
If no

Thank them, move on

You say
"That's totally fine — thanks for your time today."

One clean no is a good outcome too — log it and dial the next one.

Card 8 · The Routine
Text & Notes Routine
Same four steps, every call

Edit → Copy → Send → Paste to notes

Open your Notes app, edit the template with their name
Copy the message
Paste it into your text app and send
Paste the same message into the completion note in FieldEdge

This is how we know exactly what was sent to who — do it every time, no exceptions.
